Navigating the Pages District Overall:  High-level summary comparing the impact of all scenarios across the whole district.  Building Capacity / Utilization:  Shows which school buildings are operating at optimal student size.  Grade-Level Pages (K-5, K-8, 6-8, 9-12):  Specific enrollment projections and distance metrics for each grade band.   textbox   Welcome to the District Rightsizing & School Boundary Proposal Dashboard 
  textbox   Our Goal:  Ensure every student in our district attends a school that is sustainable, safely staffed, and packed with the academic, artistic, and support programs they deserve. 
  shape 'Link to School One Pagers'  
  pageNavigator   
  image   
  textbox   Key Definitions & Glossary  Sustainable School A school with enough enrolled students to avoid staffing changes, sustain Arts/Music and other pathways and has dedicated Social/Emotional Support Staff Utilization Rate Operating a school building within its ideal capacity (typically 70%-100%).  Prevents both uncomfortable overcrowding and underused, empty classrooms. 100% does not mean every room is filled all the time.  Status Quo Keeping all school boundaries, grade structures, and open buildings exactly as they are today. Serves as the baseline to compare proposed changes against doing nothing. Feeder Pattern The pathway determining which middle and high school students move to after elementary school. Shows your child's long-term academic pathway from K through 12th grade. Split Feeder When students graduating from one elementary school split and go to two different middle schools base
